Birds of Heaven

Birds of heaven soar so high,
Wings outstretched against the sky,
Their songs a gentle, calming breeze,
A melody that puts hearts at ease.
‍
They dance on winds that lift them near,
A peaceful grace we hold so dear,
For in their flight, both bright and true,
Is a world of hope that feels brand new.
